## Included Enterprise module

- `enterprise-ai` `0.2.7`

## Update behavior

Enterprise installations use their dedicated signed update manifest. Before
switching application containers, the updater pulls the Enterprise image pair,
verifies each Cosign signature against the Enterprise release workflow identity,
and persists the verified immutable digests to `.env`.

The update keeps Community data services and volumes intact. A failed pull,
signature verification, or backend health check does not change the saved image
references.
